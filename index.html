Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@PixelTapTV

PixelTap Uploader

PixelTap Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the PixelTapTV channel. It takes gameplay recordings from the owner's own Google Drive folder, improves the video (straightening, light sharpening, sound levelling, an episode number), writes the title and description, and uploads the video to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
